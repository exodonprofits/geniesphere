Gordon Growth Model Formula:
V = D₁ / (r - g)
Where:
V = Intrinsic Value
D₁ = Expected Dividend Next Year
r = Required Rate of Return (%)
g = Dividend Growth Rate (%)

How to Find Dividend Information

  1. Financial Information Websites:
    Visit these reliable sources:
    • Yahoo Finance
    • Morningstar
    • NASDAQ
    • Bloomberg
  2. Steps to Find Data:
    • Search for the company stock symbol
    • Go to the "Statistics" or "Analysis" section
    • Look for Dividend Yield and Growth Rates
    • Check historical dividend payments
  3. Key Information to Collect:
    • Current annual dividend per share
    • 5-year average dividend growth rate
    • Analyst growth rate projections
    • Company's dividend payout history
  4. Notes:
    • Use conservative growth estimates
    • Verify data from multiple sources
    • Consider historical averages and future projections
