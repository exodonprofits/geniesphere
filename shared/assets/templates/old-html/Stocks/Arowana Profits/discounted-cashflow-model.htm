Stock Intrinsic Value Calculator

(Uses the Discounted Cash Flow (DCF) model)

📘 How to Find Input Values (Click to Expand)

  • Stock Ticker: Find on Yahoo Finance or your brokerage platform
    (e.g., AAPL for Apple)
  • Free Cash Flow (FCF):
    • Find in company's 10-K filing (Cash Flow Statement)
    • Calculate: Operating Cash Flow - Capital Expenditures
    • Source: SEC Edgar
  • Expected Growth Rate:
    • Use analyst estimates (Yahoo Finance > Analysis tab)
    • Historical 5-year FCF growth rate
    • Typically 2-15% (be conservative)
  • Discount Rate:
    • Use company's WACC (find on GuruFocus)
    • Default: 8-12% (10% for stable companies)
  • Terminal Growth Rate:
    • Shouldn't exceed long-term GDP growth (2-4%)
  • Outstanding Shares:
    • Find in 10-K filings or Yahoo Finance > Statistics
    • Use diluted shares outstanding
