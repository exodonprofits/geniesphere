GenieSphere User Guide

Welcome to GenieSphere! This quick guide will help you get started and make the most out of your AI-powered dashboard.

Step 1: Explore the Dashboard

The dashboard is organized into three main sections:

  • Personal Tools: Smart Travel, Nutrition, Bill Tracker, Virtual Concierge
  • Business Tools: Salon Genie, Email Automator, Marketing & Payroll
  • Investment Tools: Market Intelligence & AI trading assistants

Step 2: Launch an Agent

Click “Launch Agent” under any tool card to start using that assistant. Some tools may require tokens (coming soon).

Step 3: Manage Your Tokens

Once token usage is enabled:

  • Check your balance from the wallet section (top-right of the dashboard)
  • Buy tokens in bundles via the Token Store
  • View past activity in Token Usage History

Tips for New Users

  • Use personal tools daily to save time with travel, food, or scheduling
  • Business tools are ideal for entrepreneurs or small business owners
  • Investing tools are great for learning and monitoring stock trends
