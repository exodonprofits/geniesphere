Frequently Asked Questions

What is GenieSphere?

GenieSphere is a suite of AI-powered tools designed to simplify your life and business operations. It includes agents for travel planning, marketing, payroll, investing, and more.

Is GenieSphere free?

Yes! During the initial launch phase, most agents are available for free. We will introduce a token-based access model in the future for premium tools.

What are tokens and how do they work?

Tokens are digital credits used to access GenieSphere agents. For example, launching a premium agent might cost 1–3 tokens depending on its complexity.

How can I buy tokens?

Visit the Token Store to purchase tokens securely. We offer bundles with various pricing options.

Can I use GenieSphere on mobile?

Yes, GenieSphere is mobile-friendly. You can also save it to your home screen for app-like access.

Is my data private?

We take data privacy seriously. Please refer to our Privacy Policy for full details.

How can I contact support?

You can reach out through our Contact Us page or email us at contact@geniesphere.com.
