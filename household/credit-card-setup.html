How GenieSphere uses your card info

Safe by design: we never store full card numbers or CVV. Only the basics needed for smart recommendations.

  • ✅ We store only issuer, card type, nickname, business link, optional last 4, and notes.
  • 🛡️ No full card numbers, no CVV, no expiration dates are ever stored.
  • 🧠 The optimizer uses this info to decide which card to use for groceries, utilities, travel, software, and more.
  • 🔁 You can edit or add more cards later as your wallet changes.
