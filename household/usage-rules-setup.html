How rules work

Each rule is “Category + Scope → Card”. You can change them anytime as your wallet or businesses change.

  • 🧠 Personal scope rules cover things like groceries, gas, streaming, and personal travel.
  • 📂 Business scope rules are tied to a specific business profile when possible (e.g., Victoria Nails, Exodon Profits).
  • ✅ Picking a card for a category will create or update a single rule. Choosing “No preferred card” will remove it.
  • 🔄 The Credit Card Optimizer page reads these rules to build your cheat sheet and business map.
Suggested flow 1) Add your cards → 2) Set usage rules here → 3) Print or save the PDF cheatsheet from the Optimizer. Repeat when you add or close a card.
