Agent Name: Bill Manager

Description: Stay financially organized with AI that tracks bills, sends reminders, categorizes expenses, and helps you plan smarter household and business payments with full calendar integration.

Features:

  • Track all household and business bills (mortgage, rent, utilities, insurance, car payments, subscriptions)

  • Attach receipts or invoices for each bill entry automatically
  • Multi-property or multi-business support
