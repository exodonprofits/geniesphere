✈️ Amex Platinum Travel Only
Personal

Use Platinum only when traveling: flights (except when you specifically want United or AA miles), hotels, and car rentals. This is where you unlock lounge access, travel credits, and 5× points. Avoid using it for groceries, gas, or utilities.

💙 Amex Blue Cash Preferred Daily Driver
Personal
Groceries (6%) Streaming (6%) Gas (3%) Internet Most utilities

Make this your default daily personal card. Use it for groceries, streaming services, gas, home internet, and most household utilities unless Chase Freedom is offering a 5% bonus category that fits.

🔁 Chase Freedom 5% Rotating
Personal
Gas (bonus quarters) Groceries / Amazon PayPal / Wholesale clubs

Only use Freedom when the current quarter offers 5% on something you already spend on (gas, groceries, Amazon, PayPal, wholesale clubs). Activate the bonus each quarter. Otherwise, keep this card mostly parked.

✈️ United & AA Personal Flights Situational

For personal United flights, you can use your United Club Business card if you want lounge access and free bags. For American Airlines flights, use Citi AAdvantage if you want AA miles and baggage perks. If you just want flexible points and perks, use Amex Platinum instead.

🧾 Personal Utilities & Bills
Internet & streaming → BCP Electricity & water → BCP / Freedom Cell phone → Freedom (for protection)

Use Blue Cash Preferred for internet and streaming (6%) and for most home utilities. If Freedom has a 5% quarter that covers utilities or PayPal and you can route the bill through, switch to Freedom for that quarter. For phone protection, consider paying your cell bill with a Chase card that includes device coverage.

S-Corp
Supplies Utilities Marketing Training & classes

Treat this as the single “everything” card for 3D Brows. It earns 1.5% on all spend and keeps every microblading expense in one clean lane.

🤖 Exodon Profits – Chase Ink Business Cash AI & SaaS Hub
LLC
Internet (5%) Phone (5%) Software / APIs / SaaS (5%) Marketing & ads Gas for ATM service (2%)

Put every Exodon subscription, telecom bill, and online tool on this card. It’s your highest-earning card for software and AI costs. Never use any credit card for ATM vault cash – always use your business checking.

🤖 Exodon Profits – Travel on United Situational

When you travel for Exodon (client visits, conferences), book United flights with United Club Business to earn miles and enjoy lounge access and baggage benefits.

📈 Arowana Profits – Amex Blue Business Plus Core Card
LLC
Trading software Market data AI tools Hosting & coaching platforms

Use (or open) this card as Arowana’s dedicated card. It earns 2× Membership Rewards points on all trading and coaching expenses, which you can redeem for travel with United, American partners, or other airlines.

📈 Arowana Profits – Travel Strategy

For Arowana business travel, you can book United flights with United Club Business for lounge + bags, or use Amex Platinum for premium cabins and non-United routes. Just keep everything labeled under Arowana for clean records.

Simple rule: each business uses one primary card for operations, plus United Club Business for United travel. This keeps taxes easy and lets you see each business’s spending at a glance.
