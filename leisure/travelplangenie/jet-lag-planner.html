⚡ Quick Rules
☀️Light is your strongest tool. Seek it when you want to feel awake, avoid it when you want to sleep earlier.
🌙Stay awake until local bedtime on arrival day. Naps over 20 min make it worse.
💧Drink 250ml water per hour of flight. Dehydration amplifies every jet lag symptom.
🍽️Eat at local meal times from day one — your gut clock anchors your rhythm.
🏃Morning exercise outdoors at your destination is the best free reset tool.
🔄 East vs West

Eastward (NY→London) is harder. Expect ~1 day per hour of difference.

Westward (London→NY) is easier. Usually ~0.5 days per hour.
