⚡ Golden Rules
🏥Medical cover is non-negotiable. A single night in a US hospital can cost $10,000+. Never travel without it.
📋Declare pre-existing conditions. Failing to declare means your claim can be rejected — even for unrelated events.
📅Buy before you depart. Most policies don't cover events that happen before purchase — including airline bankruptcies.
🧗Check activity exclusions. Standard policies often exclude hiking above 3,000m, scuba, and skiing — add adventure cover if needed.
📱Save your policy number. Screenshot it. Store it offline. You'll need it in an emergency when you have no internet.
⚠️ Always read the policy wording before purchasing. Exclusions vary significantly between insurers. This guide is for comparison purposes and is not financial advice.
