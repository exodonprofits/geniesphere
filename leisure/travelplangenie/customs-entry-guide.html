📋 Key Customs Rules
💵Cash over $10,000 (or equivalent) must be declared in almost every country — failure can mean confiscation.
🥩Fresh meat, fruit and plants are prohibited or restricted in most countries — especially Australia, NZ, USA and Canada.
💊Carry a doctor's letter for any prescription medication, especially controlled substances. Some countries require advance approval.
🚁Drones require permits in many countries. Some (Saudi Arabia, India, Bhutan) effectively ban recreational drone use.
