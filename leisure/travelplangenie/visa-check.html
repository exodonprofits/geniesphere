      💡 Key Things to Know
      📘Most countries require 6 months passport validity beyond your entry or return date.
      🎫Bring proof of onward/return ticket and hotel bookings — border officers can ask.
      💳Show sufficient funds — typically $50–100 USD per day equivalent depending on destination.
      ✈️Transit through certain countries can trigger separate transit visa requirements — even for a layover.
      💉Yellow Fever vaccination proof required if arriving from certain affected countries.
      🌍Schengen area uses the 90/180 day rule — count all recent days across all member countries.
      📲Many destinations now require a digital arrival form (e.g. SG Arrival Card, ESTA) before boarding.
