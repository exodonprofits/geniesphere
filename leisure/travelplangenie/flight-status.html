💡 If Your Flight Is Disrupted
✅ Delayed under 2 hours Check the airline app for updated gate info. Grab water and food — delays often extend. Don't leave the gate area.
⚠️ Delayed 2+ hours You may be entitled to a meal voucher. Ask at the gate. If connecting, ask the agent to rebook your connection proactively.
🚫 Cancelled flight Call the airline's reservations line (hold times are shorter than gate lines). Use the airline app to rebook. EU passengers: EC261 entitles you to compensation for cancellations within airline control.
💳 Travel insurance & credit cards Many premium credit cards cover trip delay, interruption, and missed connections. File a claim with your card's travel protection — keep all receipts.
