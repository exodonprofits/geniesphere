💡 Travel Money Tips
✅ Best exchange Use ATMs abroad in local currency. Avoid airport kiosks — they typically charge 10–15% above mid-market rate.
💳 Credit cards Visa/Mastercard use the mid-market rate + 1–3% fee. Cards like Wise, Revolut, or Charles Schwab charge no foreign transaction fees.
⚠️ DCC trap When paying abroad, always choose "charge in local currency" — not your home currency. Dynamic Currency Conversion adds 3–8% markup.
📱 Wise / Revolut For frequent travelers, multi-currency accounts give you mid-market rates with minimal fees — worth getting before any trip.
