❓ Frequently Asked Questions
Is Voyage Genie free to use? ▼
Yes — generating itineraries, using the planners, and saving trips are all free. We earn a small affiliate commission when you book through our partner links, at no extra cost to you.
How does the AI itinerary generator work? ▼
You fill in your trip preferences — destination, dates, budget, group size — and our AI builds a personalised day-by-day itinerary with real booking links. It uses your budget tier to recommend flights, hotels, and activities at the right price point.
Are the prices in the itinerary accurate? ▼
Prices shown are AI estimates based on typical rates for your budget tier and destination. They're a useful starting point, but always click the booking links for live pricing before you book.
Do you store my trip data? ▼
When you create an account, your saved trips are stored securely in our database. If you use the planners without signing in, your trip data is only saved in your browser's local storage. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
Which destinations does Voyage Genie cover? ▼
Voyage Genie works for virtually any destination worldwide. Our four planners cover tropical beach resorts, ocean cruises, city & cultural stays (hotels, Airbnbs), and adventure travel. If you're planning a trip somewhere, Genie can help.
How do I report a bug or request a feature? ▼
Use the contact form above and select "Bug report" or "Feature request" — we read every submission and prioritise fixes based on what affects the most users.
