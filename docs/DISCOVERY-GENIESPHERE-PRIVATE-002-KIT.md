## 1. What we are trying to learn

We are testing one hypothesis (001 §1):

> Busy multi-business / multi-property owners will pay Exodon a setup fee plus a recurring retainer for a private, maintained "household + business attention briefing", built on GenieSphere and fed by the Genies they already use. No concierge team, no bank aggregation, no advice.

The five questions, in order:

1. **Pain.** Do these people actually lose time, money or sleep coordinating obligations across homes, businesses, vehicles, vendors and family?
2. **Current spend.** What do they already pay (people, tools, time) to cope?
3. **Fit.** Would a weekly or daily "what needs my attention" briefing replace or reduce that spend?
4. **Trust.** What would they need to see before handing over this information to a small company?
5. **Willingness to pay.** Will they put down money for a pilot, not just say it sounds nice?

## 2. Who to talk to

### 2.1 Qualifying screen

Run this before booking, in a text or 2-minute call. Qualify only if **at least 3 of the 6** apply:

| # | Criterion |
|---|---|
| 1 | Owns or runs **2+ businesses**, or 1 business with 10+ staff |
| 2 | Owns **2+ properties** (primary home + rental/vacation/commercial) |
| 3 | **3+ vehicles**, boats or other insured high-value assets |
| 4 | Pays **someone** to help coordinate: EA, bookkeeper doing personal bills, house manager, family member |
| 5 | Works with **3+ advisers**: CPA, attorney, insurance broker, wealth manager |
| 6 | Has **missed a renewal, payment, filing or deadline in the last 12 months** that cost money or stress |

Record their segment: **B** (multi-business owner) or **C** (multi-property family). Family offices (Segment D) are out of scope. Politely skip them.

### 2.2 Sources

| Source | Target count | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Founder's personal and business network | 5–6 | Fastest. Watch for "being nice to a friend" bias; push for past behaviour |
| Exodon Profits clients | 3–4 | Already trust you; ideal for the pilot |
| Salon Genie / EasyNailSpa salon owners with multiple locations or properties | 2–3 | Segment B |
| Referral partners (CPA, estate attorney, insurance broker) | 2–3 partners | Each may introduce 1–3 clients. Use the §5 partner script |

**Goal: 12–15 qualified interviews.** Stop early if the kill criteria in §8 are clearly met.

## 3. Interview script (30–40 min)

**Principles.** Ask about the past, not the future. Specifics, not opinions. Don't pitch until the last 5 minutes. Silence is fine.

> "Would you use X?" is worthless. "Tell me about the last time X happened" is gold.

### 3.1 Opening (2 min)

> "Thanks for making time. I'm researching how busy owners keep track of everything across their businesses, homes and family. Nothing to sell today. I'm trying to learn how it actually works for you. Mind if I take notes? I won't write down account numbers or anything sensitive."

### 3.2 Map their world (5 min)

1. "Walk me through what you're responsible for: businesses, properties, vehicles, family stuff."
2. "Who helps you keep it all running? Anyone paid, or a spouse or family member?"
3. "What tools do you use day to day to keep track? Calendar, spreadsheets, apps, notes, email folders?"

*Note counts only: number of businesses, properties, vehicles, staff and advisers.*

### 3.3 The last time it went wrong (10 min, the most important part)

4. "Tell me about the last time something slipped through the cracks. A renewal, bill, registration, inspection, filing, appointment."
   - "What happened? How did you find out?"
   - "What did it cost? Money, late fees, time, stress, a relationship?"
   - "What did you change afterwards, if anything?"
5. "What's the thing you worry most about forgetting right now?"
6. "How much time a week do you, or someone you pay, spend just keeping track of what's due?"

### 3.4 Current spend and workarounds (7 min)

7. "What do you pay today for help with this? People, services, software, rough monthly or yearly."
8. "Have you tried an app or service for this and stopped using it? Why did you stop?"
9. "If your assistant or bookkeeper left tomorrow, what would break first?"

### 3.5 Trust (5 min)

10. "Who has access to your full picture today: insurance, property, vehicles, business deadlines?"
11. "What would a company need to show you before you'd let them organize that information?"
12. "Anything you'd never put into an app, no matter who made it?"

### 3.6 Reaction to the concept (5 min, only now)

Describe it in **one sentence**, no screens:

> "Imagine every Monday, and whenever something is urgent, you get one short briefing: what's due this week across your businesses, homes, vehicles and family, what needs your decision, and what's already handled. Someone on our side sets it up and keeps it current."

13. "Where would that help, and where wouldn't it?"
14. "What would it have to include to be worth your time?"
15. "Who else should I talk to who has this problem worse than you?" *(referral)*

### 3.7 The ask (1 min)

Only ask if they scored pain ≥ 4 (§6).

> "We're running a small paid pilot with three households this quarter. Would you want to hear what that looks like?"

If yes, send the one-page pilot offer (§4) the same day.

## 4. Pilot offer (one page, private)

**Do not post this anywhere public. Fill in the bracketed values before each conversation.** The ranges are 001 §7 hypotheses, not decided prices.

---

### GenieSphere Private Pilot — by Exodon Profits

**For:** owners juggling multiple businesses, properties and family obligations.

**What you get, over 8 weeks:**

1. **Setup session (90 min).** We map your obligations: renewals, registrations, insurance, property maintenance, business deadlines, recurring vendors, family dates.
2. **We build your inventory.** You hand us documents or a list. We organize it in a private, isolated workspace that only you and the Exodon setup lead can see.
3. **Weekly attention briefing.** One short email or text every Monday: due this week, needs your decision, coming up in 30 days, already handled.
4. **Urgent alerts.** Only when something is due within 72 hours and not marked handled.
5. **Monthly 30-min review** to adjust what matters.

**What we do not do:**

- move money or pay bills
- log into your bank or brokerage
- give investment, tax or legal advice
- make bookings or calls on your behalf

**What we never collect:**

- passwords or account logins
- full card, Social Security or ID numbers
- medical records
- safe or alarm codes

**Your data:**

- Kept separate from every other client.
- Exportable and deletable on request, and deleted at the end of the pilot if you don't continue.

**Pilot price:**

- setup: [$____]
- [$____] per month for 2 months
- deposit of [$____], refundable if you cancel before the setup session

**After the pilot:** continue month to month, or stop. No obligation.

---

**Hypothesis bands to choose from (internal only):**

- Setup: $2,500–5,000
- Retainer: $300–800/mo
- Deposit: 25–50% of setup

Pick one price per prospect and record it in the log. Varying it across prospects tests price sensitivity.

## 5. Referral-partner script (CPA / estate attorney / insurance broker)

20 minutes.

1. "Which of your clients have the messiest picture: multiple entities, properties, vehicles, advisers?"
2. "When something gets missed, a renewal, an estimated tax payment, a registration, how do you usually find out, and who cleans it up?"
3. "Do clients ask you to help with that coordination today? Do you bill for it or eat it?"
4. "Would you refer a client to a service that keeps their obligations organized and sends them a weekly briefing? What would make you comfortable doing that?"
5. "What security or credentials would you need to see before referring?"
   - Record whether they say SOC 2 or a similar formal audit. If so, see the §8 HOLD rule.
6. "Anyone you'd introduce me to this month?"

## 6. Scoring each interview

Fill this in right after each interview, from what they said and did, not from how polite they were.

| Field | Scale / values |
|---|---|
| Pain severity | 1 = shrug, 3 = annoying, 5 = cost real money or caused a crisis in the last 12 months |
| Frequency | 1 = rarely, 3 = monthly, 5 = weekly or more |
| Current spend on coordination | $/month (people + tools), or "time only" |
| Concrete past incident? | Yes / No (and the cost if yes) |
| Tried and abandoned a tool? | Name + reason |
| Trust requirement | None stated / references / isolation & deletion / formal audit (SOC 2) |
| Never-store items they named | Free text |
| Concept reaction | 1 = no interest, 3 = "interesting", 5 = asked how to start |
| Pilot ask | Not asked / declined / wants offer / deposit paid |
| Price quoted | $ setup / $ month |
| Referrals given | Count |

## 8. Go / kill rules

These come from 001 §17. Decide against these numbers, not against feelings.

| Gate | Pass | Kill | Hold |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Interviews** (after 12–15) | ≥ 6 with pain ≥ 4 **and** coordination spend > $0 | < 3 with pain ≥ 4 | — |
| **Pre-sale** (60 days) | ≥ 2 paid deposits | 0 deposits after 8+ qualified offers | — |
| **Pilot value** (6 weeks) | ≥ 2 of 3 would be upset to lose it | 0 of 3 | — |
| **Pilot cost** | ≤ 2 hrs/household/week after setup | Time does not fall after week 3 | — |
| **Trust** | Segment B accepts isolation + deletion + references | — | Segment B also requires a formal SOC 2 audit → HOLD until a SOC 2 path is funded |
| **Channel** | ≥ 1 partner willing to refer | — | All partners require SOC 2 first → HOLD |

**Outcome mapping for the 002 result document:**

| Gate result | Outcome |
|---|---|
| All pass | **GO** on the Exodon service model. Feed learnings into the GenieSphere Core roadmap (briefing, permissions, Home). Still no separate tier |
| Interviews pass, pre-sale fails | **MODIFY.** Pain is real but the offer or price is wrong. One more iteration only |
| Interviews fail | **REJECT** GenieSphere Private. Keep the attention briefing as a core GenieSphere feature |
| Trust gate triggers HOLD | **HOLD** |
