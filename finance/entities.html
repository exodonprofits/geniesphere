❓ What is an entity?

An entity is a business or legal unit you want to track separately — like an LLC, corporation, partnership, nonprofit, or sole proprietorship. Finance Core uses entities to keep records clean, prevent commingling, and power multi‑entity views later.

Next phases add account connections (bank/credit/brokerage) and entity relationships (management fees, loans, ownership).
